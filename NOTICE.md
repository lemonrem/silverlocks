# Notice

Silverlocks is an independent, hook-free adaptation inspired by the MIT-licensed [Goldilocks](https://github.com/blackstone2333/goldilocks) project.

The workflow was redesigned as a standalone Codex Skill: plugin and Hook machinery were removed, continuity became a bounded replace-only snapshot with explicit archives, planning and verification were made proportional, and repository-specific assumptions were removed.

Silverlocks is not affiliated with or endorsed by the Goldilocks maintainers or OpenAI.

The 0.2.3 workflow revision draws on Goldilocks 0.6.1 (`a4bd31b`), particularly evidence-based diagnosis, failed-hypothesis memory, execution return points, and single-owner handoffs. These ideas are adapted to Silverlocks's existing authorization, repository-first continuity, and no-Hook design; Goldilocks model routing and fixed receipts are not bundled.
